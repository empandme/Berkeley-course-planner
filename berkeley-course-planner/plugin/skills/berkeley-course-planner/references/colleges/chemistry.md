# College of Chemistry (CoC)

The catalog states CoC college requirements on every College of Chemistry program page: open the Requirements tab, then click a set name under "College Requirements" to open its text. The links below use the Chemical Biology BS page (shared sets) and the Chemical Engineering BS page (its own breadth set). Breadth differs by major: Chemistry and Chemical Biology use "COC Breadth"; Chemical Engineering uses "COC Breadth: Chemical Engineering".

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 13 units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- Maximum per semester: 20.5 units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- Chemical Engineering freshmen and Chemistry majors must enroll in at least one chemistry course each semester; after the freshman year, Chemical Engineering majors must enroll in at least one chemical engineering course each semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Exceeding the maximum: students in good academic standing may request permission from their College Adviser; if approved, they may add the extra units beginning the Monday of the second week of classes, not before [src: https://chemistry.berkeley.edu/node/407 (section "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- Reduced Course Load (approved by DSP or for other reasons): an RCL of 8-12 units must include at least 2 required technical courses; an RCL of 7 or fewer units must include at least 1 [src: https://chemistry.berkeley.edu/node/407 (section "Class Schedule Requirements")]

## Breadth

- Chemistry and Chemical Biology majors: 15 units of humanities and social sciences breadth in total, including the Reading & Composition and American Cultures courses [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Breadth")]
- The remaining units must come from these L&S breadth areas: Arts and Literature, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy and Values, Social and Behavioral Sciences; courses that only teach a skill (such as drawing or playing an instrument) do not count [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Breadth")]
- Breadth courses may be taken Pass/No Pass, except Reading and Composition [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Breadth")]
- AP, IB and GCE A-level exam credit may be used to satisfy breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Breadth")]
- Chemical Engineering majors: 22 units of humanities and social sciences breadth in total, including the Reading & Composition and American Cultures courses [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/10294U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Breadth: Chemical Engineering")]
- Chemical Engineering majors: the 22 units include a two-course Breadth Series, at least one upper division, in the same or a very closely allied humanities or social science department(s); the remaining units come from the same five L&S areas (skill-only courses excluded); P/NP allowed except for Reading and Composition; AP, IB and GCE A-level exam credit may be used [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/10294U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Breadth: Chemical Engineering")]
- Passed/Not Passed is open to students in good standing for breadth (not Reading and Composition), the foreign language requirement, free electives, and American History and Institutions or American Cultures courses; students on academic notice may not take courses P/NP (except recreational PE and P/NP-only courses) [src: https://chemistry.berkeley.edu/node/407 (section "Passed/Not Passed Courses")]

## Reading & Composition

- Two semesters of lower division composition, Part A and Part B, taken in sequential order [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Every course used for Part A or Part B needs a letter grade of C- or better [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Deadline: all undergraduates should complete Reading and Composition by the end of their fourth semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Chemical Engineering: an A-level Reading and Composition course (e.g., English R1A) is required by the end of the first year [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/10294U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements, note under the sets)]
- Chemical Biology and Chemistry majors: the A- and B-level Reading and Composition courses by the end of the second year [src: https://chemistry.berkeley.edu/node/407 (section "Class Schedule Requirements", "Reading & Composition")]
- A student without Entry Level Writing must enroll in College Writing R1A (6 units), which satisfies Entry Level Writing and Part A; Entry Level Writing needs a C or higher in it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Exams: AP English Literature 4 satisfies Part A and 5 satisfies Parts A and B; AP English Language 4 or 5 satisfies Part A; IB HL English A: Literature 5 to 7 satisfies Parts A and B, and for students admitted fall 2018 or later so does IB HL English A: Language and Literature 5 to 7; GCE A-Level English Literature is not accepted [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Full CalGETC/UC IGETC Certification or UC Reciprocity satisfies Reading and Composition for students admitted to the College of Chemistry [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]

## Other college requirements

- Degree-applicable units each semester (a separate rule from the 13-unit minimum load): 12 units of coursework each semester must satisfy degree requirements [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- At least 120 semester units; at least a C average (2.0 GPA) in all courses undertaken at UC (including UC Summer Sessions, UCEAP, UCDC and University Extension XB courses) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Semester limit: 8 semesters for students who entered as freshmen; transfers get 4 (Chemistry and Chemical Biology) or 5 (Chemical Engineering and joint majors); summer sessions do not count; delaying graduation for a minor, double major or simultaneous degrees must be approved before what would normally be the final two semesters [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Limit")]
- The College of Chemistry has no rule on the maximum units a student can accumulate [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Limit")]
- Senior residence: after 90 units, at least 24 of the remaining units in residence in the College of Chemistry over at least two semesters; a semester counts if it includes at least 4 units of successfully completed courses, and a summer session can count [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Senior Residence")]
- A grade of D+ or lower in a chemistry or chemical engineering course that requires C- or higher means the course must be repeated at Berkeley [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Minimum Course Grade Requirements")]
- Sequence grades: C- or higher in CHEM 4A before CHEM 4B, in CHEM 4B before more advanced courses, and in CHEM 12A before CHEM 12B; a GPA of at least 2.0 in all courses taken in the college to advance to and continue in the upper division [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Minimum Course Grade Requirements")]
- Chemistry and Chemical Biology majors also need C- or higher in CHEM 120A and CHEM 120B if taken before CHEM 125 or CHEM C182 [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/101A1U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COC Unit and Academic Requirements", "Minimum Course Grade Requirements")]
