# Haas School of Business (Haas)

The catalog states Haas college requirements on the Business Administration BS page: open the Requirements tab, then click a set name under "College Requirements" ("Haas Seven Course Breadth", "Haas Academic Requirements") to open its text. Neither set gives per-semester unit numbers or Reading and Composition rules, so those come from the Haas website, the Haas 4-Year Degree Requirements PDF, the program page's "Entry Level Writing (ELWR)" dialog and the catalog's campus-wide Reading and Composition page, which never names Haas. The program page also shows "General Guidelines" text outside the set dialogs. Haas states a semester minimum but no semester maximum.

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 13 units; graduating seniors may take fewer than 13 in their final term if all graduation requirements are met; enrolling in fewer than 13 results in an advising hold in CalCentral [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Minimum Units Per Semester")]
- Maximum per semester: not stated by Haas; its Academic Policies page sets the 13-unit minimum and no maximum [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Minimum Units Per Semester")]
- During the Adjustment Period students may enroll up to their college's maximum unit limit; continuing undergraduates are capped at 17.5 units in Phase 2 [src: https://registrar.berkeley.edu/enrollment/]
- Normal degree progress includes successful completion of at least 13 units per semester, except possibly in the semester of graduation [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Normal Degree Progress, Academic Notice and Disqualification")]
- Note — college site differs: campus.md's Special Studies caps cover courses numbered 97, 98, 99, 197, 198 and 199, but Haas's "Passed/Not Passed Option" text says students may not receive more than four units of "98, 99, 197, 198, and 199 courses" in a single semester (to enroll in more, e-mail UGMajorAdvising@haas.berkeley.edu) and that no more than 16 units total in courses numbered 98, 99, 197, 198, 199 count toward degree requirements [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Passed/Not Passed Option")]
- Note — college site differs: Haas's "Enrollment in Courses" text lists different course numbers for the same 4-unit and 16-unit caps: no more than 4 units of 97, 98, 197, 198, or 199 per semester, and no more than 16 units total of 98, 198, or 199 toward degree requirements [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Enrollment in Courses")]

## Breadth

- Seven Course Breadth is required for the BS: Arts and Literature, Biological Science, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy and Values, Physical Science, Social and Behavioral Sciences; Haas students follow the L&S 7-course breadth guidelines with the exceptions below [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Seven Course Breadth")]
- Business courses and Reading and Composition courses cannot be used for breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Seven Course Breadth")]
- Microeconomics and macroeconomics courses at any level cannot be used for breadth (the catalog names Econ 1, Econ 2, Econ 3, Econ 100A/B, Econ 101A/B, IAS 106/107) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Seven Course Breadth")]
- No more than two courses from any one department (L&S Discovery courses are exempt) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Seven Course Breadth")]
- AP and IB credit will not satisfy breadth; A Level exams are accepted (a Haas Adviser determines how) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Seven Course Breadth")]
- Breadth courses may be taken at a California Community College following the current ASSIST articulation agreement for the business administration major [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Seven Course Breadth")]
- Note — college site differs: the catalog's General Guidelines, shown on the same program page, say Haas students "must adhere to the 7-course breadth guidelines outlined on the Haas Undergraduate Program website" and "Breadth courses must be completed with at least a C- or P grades"; the "Haas Seven Course Breadth" dialog cited above points to the College of Letters and Science website for those guidelines [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (General Guidelines text visible on the program page, outside the set dialogs)]

## Reading & Composition

- Haas requires Part A (R1A) and Part B (R1B) of the Reading & Composition requirement [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/4-Year-Degree-Requirements.pdf]
- Haas prerequisite courses, which include Part A (R1A) and Part B (R1B) of Reading and Composition, must be finished with a letter grade of C- or higher; the PDF says students "must complete at least 60 semester units and finish all prerequisite courses with a letter grade of C- or higher" [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/4-Year-Degree-Requirements.pdf]
- Campus-wide rule (the Reading and Composition page never names Haas): the two parts are taken in sequential order, and every course used for Part A or Part B needs a letter grade of C- or better; no Haas-specific source states "sequential order" [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/college-school-requirements/reading-composition]
- Deadline: first-year admits must finish all Haas prerequisites, including Reading and Composition, by the end of the spring semester of the second year; transfer and continuing-student admits must finish them before starting at Haas [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/4-Year-Degree-Requirements.pdf]
- Note — college site differs: the campus-wide Reading and Composition page, which never names Haas, says all undergraduates should complete Reading and Composition by the end of their fourth semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/college-school-requirements/reading-composition]
- A student who has not met Entry Level Writing must complete the 6-unit COLWRIT R1A; a grade of C or higher satisfies both Entry Level Writing and Part A of Reading and Composition [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (University of California Requirements > "Entry Level Writing (ELWR)")]

## Other college requirements

- At least 120 units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Academic Requirements")]
- Completion of the degree in four or eight semesters, not including summer session [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Haas Academic Requirements")]
- Note — college site differs: the General Guidelines shown on the same program page say "Students must complete the degree program in four semesters, not including Summer Session"; the pages read do not say which of four or eight applies to a given student [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (General Guidelines text visible on the program page, outside the set dialogs)]
- Note — college site differs: Haas's Time to Graduation policy lets first-year admits petition for up to ten semesters (not more than five years after matriculation) and third-year admits up to six semesters (not more than three years) [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Time to Graduation, Leave of Absence and Readmission Policies")]
- At least 38 upper-division business units and 12 upper-division non-business units [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/4-Year-Degree-Requirements.pdf]
- Normal degree progress also requires all lower division requirements by the end of the spring of the sophomore year (or the end of the 4th semester), a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0, and a term GPA of at least 1.5 [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Normal Degree Progress, Academic Notice and Disqualification")]
- Grading: all Haas business courses, including core substitutions, must be taken for a letter grade, except UGBA 194, UGBA 198 and UGBA 199 (offered Pass/No Pass only) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/70141U/requirements-krhha (General Guidelines text visible on the program page, outside the set dialogs)]
- Business majors must take all UGBA courses (including core substitute courses) for a letter grade, except UGBA 194, 198 and 199 (offered P/NP only) [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Add/Drop/Grading Option Deadlines")]
- All students must take the first attempt of all lower division requirements and business classes for a letter grade only [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/degree-requirements/]
- The 4-Year Degree Requirements PDF says all Haas Business courses, including core substitutions, must be taken for letter grades (exceptions UGBA 194, 198 & 199) and that all prerequisite courses must be taken for a letter grade [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/4-Year-Degree-Requirements.pdf]
- Note — college site differs: the "Passed/Not Passed Option" accordion words the rule as: "ALL upper division business courses must be taken for letter grades (with the exception of UGBA 194/198/199 which are only offered P/NP), regardless of whether or not you have completed the 38 upper division business units" [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Passed/Not Passed Option")]
- Note — college site differs: Haas states its P/NP limit as no more than 1/3 of the student's total UC Berkeley units (including physical education, Education Abroad and other-UC-campus units, and excluding exam units and coursework from other colleges); campus.md words the 1/3 Passed Grade Limit on a different base (120 minus transfer and exam units) [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Passed/Not Passed Option")]
- Students on Academic Notice must enroll in at least 13 units and take all courses for letter grades [src: https://haas.berkeley.edu/undergrad/academics/curriculum/academic-policies/ (accordion "Normal Degree Progress, Academic Notice and Disqualification")]
