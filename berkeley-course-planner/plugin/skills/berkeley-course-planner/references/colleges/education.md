# Berkeley School of Education (BSE)

BSE offers the Bachelor of Science in Educational Sciences. On that program's Requirements tab the catalog says "The Berkeley School of Education requirements mirror those of the College of Letters & Science" and lists the L&S requirement sets; click a set name under "College Requirements" to open its text. The catalog gives no per-semester unit numbers, so the unit limits come from the BSE website.

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 13 units (Fall and Spring semesters) [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/policies/general-course-registrationenrollment (section "Unit Loads")]
- Maximum per semester: 20.5 units (Fall and Spring semesters) [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/policies/general-course-registrationenrollment (section "Unit Loads")]
- Fewer than 13 units needs an approved Reduced Course Load (RCL); eligible grounds are paid employment of 15 or more hours a week, DSP, parenthood (two courses totalling six units), an approved late drop, or graduating senior in the EGT semester; RCL petitions are due by the add/drop deadline in the fourth week [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/policies/general-course-registrationenrollment (section "Unit Loads")]
- An unapproved load under 13 units brings a warning email the first time and a hold on future registration the next time [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/policies/general-course-registrationenrollment (section "Unit Loads")]
- Above 20.5 units: the BSE Registration/Enrollment page gives the 20.5-unit maximum and states no procedure for exceeding it [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/policies/general-course-registrationenrollment (section "Unit Loads")]

## Breadth

- Seven-Course Breadth: one approved course in each of seven areas: Arts and Literature, Biological Science, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy and Values, Physical Science, Social and Behavioral Sciences [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Each course needs a minimum grade of C- or P [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Seven distinct courses are required; a course listed in several areas may be used for only one of them [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Only college courses count; exams such as AP and IB cannot be used for breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- At most two courses from any one academic department, counting all of that department's subject areas and, for a cross-listed ("C") course, every department offering it; Letters and Science ("LS") courses are exempt from this limit [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Courses that fulfill American History & Institutions, American Cultures, or major requirements may also count toward Seven-Course Breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- IGETC Certification or UC Reciprocity satisfies Seven-Course Breadth; a transfer course needs C- or better (or the host campus's Passed equivalent) and at least 3.0 semester or 4.0 quarter units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]

## Reading & Composition

- Two semesters of lower division composition, Part A and Part B, taken in sequential order [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Every course used for Part A or Part B needs a letter grade of C- or better [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Deadline: all undergraduates should complete Reading and Composition by the end of their fourth semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- A student without Entry Level Writing must enroll in College Writing R1A (6 units), which satisfies Entry Level Writing and Part A; Entry Level Writing needs a C or higher in it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- AGRS R44 with C- or higher may satisfy either Part A or Part B; no other Reading and Composition course may count toward Seven-Course Breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Exams: AP English Literature 4 satisfies Part A and 5 satisfies Parts A and B; AP English Language 4 or 5 satisfies Part A; IB HL English A: Literature 5 to 7 satisfies Parts A and B, and for students admitted fall 2018 or later so does IB HL English A: Language and Literature 5 to 7 (IB SL exams and the IB Literature and Performance exam are not eligible); GCE A-Level English Literature is not accepted [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Full CalGETC/UC IGETC Certification or UC Reciprocity satisfies Reading and Composition for students admitted to the School of Education [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]

## Other college requirements

- Quantitative Reasoning (L&S set): one approved course or a qualifying exam score [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Quantitative Reasoning")]
- Language (L&S set): proficiency in a language other than English equivalent to the second-semester college level, by exam or approved course; a Berkeley course needs a letter grade of C- or higher; IGETC Certification or UC Reciprocity satisfies it, CalGETC does not [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Language Requirement")]
- Units (L&S set): 120 total; 36 upper division; of those 36, 6 in courses offered outside the major department and 18 taken in residence [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/79249U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Unit Requirements")]
- The 6 outside upper division units must be outside the Education department; no more than 16 units from courses numbered 97, 98, 99, 197, 198 or 199 apply to the degree [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/undergrad-major/bse-undergraduate-degree-requirements (section "Unit Requirements and Restrictions")]
- Note — college site differs: BSE states its 1/3 Passed (P) Grade Limit as no more than one third of the total units completed at UC Berkeley (including FPF, UCEAP or UCDC) toward the 120, with transfer credit not factored in; campus.md words the 1/3 Passed Grade Limit on a different base (120 minus transfer and exam units) [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/undergrad-major/bse-undergraduate-degree-requirements (section "Unit Requirements and Restrictions")]
- Senior residence: 2 terms of residence at the Berkeley School of Education; a Summer Session can count as one term with at least 6 units passed if the student is enrolled in the BSE [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/undergrad-major/bse-undergraduate-degree-requirements (section "Senior Residence")]
- Term limit: eight semesters for first-year entrants and four for transfers (fall and spring only); one more semester for a simultaneous degree [src: https://bse.berkeley.edu/academics/undergraduate-programs/undergrad-major/bse-undergraduate-degree-requirements (section "BSE Term Limit")]
