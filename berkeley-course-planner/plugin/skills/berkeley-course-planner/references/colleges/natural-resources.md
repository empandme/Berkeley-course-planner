# Rausser College of Natural Resources (RCNR)

The catalog states RCNR college requirements on every RCNR program page: open the Requirements tab, then click a set name under "College Requirements" to open its text. The links below use the Society and Environment BS page and the Environmental Economics and Policy BS page. The college's own policies are in the "RCNR OISA Policies Document", a Google Doc linked from nature.berkeley.edu/current-students (cited here in its read-only view).

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 12 units (each fall and spring semester, unless on an approved Reduced Courseload) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Unit Minimum")]
- Maximum per semester: 20.5 units; to take more, review RCNR's Exceeding Maximum Unit Limits policies [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Unit Maximum")]
- Going outside the 12 to 20.5 unit range each semester requires an exception approved by the College [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Enrollment Minimums & Maximums")]
- Reduced Course Loads may be granted for dependent care, disability, employment or graduation [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Reduced Course Loads")]
- Exceeding 20.5: 21-24.5 units needs a prior semester of 17-20 units with at least a 3.0 term GPA and College Advisor approval; 25-29 units needs a prior semester of 24 units with at least a 3.0 term GPA and approval from the Assistant Dean of Instruction & Student Affairs; no load above 29 units; approved units may be added from the Monday of the second week of instruction [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Exceeding Maximum Unit Limits")]

## Breadth

- The RCNR policies list the Rausser College requirements as Reading and Composition (R&C), Term Limits, College Residency and Other College-Specific Requirements; breadth is not among them [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Rausser College Requirements")]
- The catalog's College Requirements for the Society and Environment BS list only "Reading and Composition" and "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements" (no breadth set) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements)]
- The catalog's College Requirements for the Environmental Economics and Policy BS add a third set, "Environmental Economics & Policy Major Breadth Requirements" [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/04779U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements)]
- That EEP set is Seven-Course Breadth: one course in each of Arts and Literature, Biological Science, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy and Values, Physical Science, Social and Behavioral Sciences, each with C- or P; seven distinct courses; at most two from any one academic department (LS courses exempt); AH&I, American Cultures and major courses may also count; exams such as AP and IB cannot be used [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/04779U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Environmental Economics & Policy Major Breadth Requirements")]
- EEP breadth courses may be taken P/NP unless they are also used for other EEP major requirements, in which case they need a letter grade [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Major Declaration Requirement")]

## Reading & Composition

- Two semesters of lower division composition, Part A and Part B, taken in sequential order [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Every course used for Part A or Part B needs a letter grade of C- or better [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Note — college site differs: the RCNR policies list a grade of C or better in approved Reading and Composition A and B courses (and in approved California community college courses) [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Reading & Composition (R&C)")]
- Deadline: all undergraduates should complete Reading and Composition by the end of their fourth semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Note — college site differs: the RCNR policies say all Rausser undergraduates must complete it by the end of their fourth term at Berkeley or the university will block registration for the following semester; no waivers [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Reading & Composition (R&C)")]
- A student without Entry Level Writing must enroll in College Writing R1A (6 units), which satisfies Entry Level Writing and Part A; Entry Level Writing needs a C or higher in it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Exams: AP English Literature 4 satisfies Part A and 5 satisfies Parts A and B; AP English Language 4 or 5 satisfies Part A; IB HL English A: Literature 5 to 7 satisfies Parts A and B, and for students admitted fall 2018 or later so does IB HL English A: Language and Literature 5 to 7; GCE A-Level English Literature is not accepted [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Note — college site differs: the RCNR policies list the "General Certificate of Education "A" Level English Exam: A, B, or C (prior to 2017 only)", although the catalog dialog does not accept GCE A-Level English Literature; they also list IB HL English A: Language and Literature 5 or higher [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic]
- IGETC certification satisfies the full Reading and Composition requirement [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Reading & Composition (R&C)")]

## Other college requirements

- Units: at least 120; at least 36 upper division, including at least 15 upper division units in the Rausser College (ENVECON, ENERES, ESPM, MBN, PLANTBI), which may be major courses or taken separately [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements", "Unit Requirements")]
- Note — college site differs: the RCNR policies list the subjects for the 15 Rausser upper division units as ENVECON, ESPM, ENERES, NUSCTX and PLANTBI, and say units not required for the major may be letter graded or Pass/Not Pass [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Other College-Specific Degree Requirements")]
- Note — college site differs: the Society and Environment BS program page's General Guidelines list the subjects for the 15 Rausser upper division units as "ENVECON, ENE,RES, ESPM, NATRES, NUSCTX, PLANTBI" (the page prints a comma inside "ENE,RES") [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (program page's General Guidelines, outside the set dialogs)]
- Unit restrictions: at most 16 units of Special Studies (97, 98, 99, 197, 198, 199) toward the 120 and at most 4 in a given semester; at most 4 units of Physical Education [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements", "Unit Requirements")]
- RCNR's own Passed (P) limit, as the RCNR set states it: unit credit for P-graded courses (including P/NP units through EAP) up to one-third of the total units taken and passed on the Berkeley campus at the time of graduation; Spring 2020 P/NP units do not count toward the limit; campus.md words the 1/3 Passed Grade Limit on a different base (120 minus transfer and exam units) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements", "Unit Requirements")]
- Note — college site differs: the RCNR policies, under "Other University-Specific Degree Requirements", say no more than one-third of the total units attempted at UC Berkeley may be taken Passed/Not Passed, including Education Abroad and UC Intercampus Visitor or Exchange units [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "Other University-Specific Degree Requirements")]
- Major requirements must be letter graded (EEP breadth excepted); a grade of at least C- in all courses for the major; a 2.0 UC GPA for graduation [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Term limit: 8 semesters for first-year entrants and 4 for junior transfers (summers excluded; the Fall Program for First Semester counts); one more semester is granted automatically for more than one major or for UCEAP/UCDC study during the academic year; no limit on total units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/043A2U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "RCNR Unit and Academic Requirements", "Term Limit")]
- College residency: at least two terms in residence in Rausser College, each with the student declared in the Rausser major before that term's grades post and at least 6 units completed; at most one of the two may be a summer term [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-Su7g7nJk8KFgEID8yCGcOez8u7_j3IrEY2ZjsLSEQw/mobilebasic (section "College Residency")]
