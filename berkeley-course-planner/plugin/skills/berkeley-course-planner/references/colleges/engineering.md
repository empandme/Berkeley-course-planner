# College of Engineering (CoE)

The catalog states CoE college requirements on every Engineering program page: open the Requirements tab, then click a set name under "College Requirements" to open its text. The links below use the Mechanical Engineering BS page; any CoE major page shows the same sets. CoE's breadth is the Humanities and Social Sciences (H/SS) requirement, and Reading and Composition is part of it.

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 12 units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements", "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- Maximum per semester: 20.5 units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements", "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- Normal progress: at least 12 units each semester (13 in the M.E.T. program), including at least two letter-graded technical courses of at least three units each (ENGIN 26 excepted); students on the degree list who are completing their requirements may take fewer than 12 [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Students out of compliance with enrollment policies by the end of the fifth week face a registration block that delays enrollment for the next semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Programs of less than 12 units or more than 20.5 units require approval; requests for more than 20.5 units are not reviewed until the second week of the semester (consult the ESS Adviser) [src: https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/policies-procedures/enrollment/#ac12250 (section "Course load")]
- Excess-unit petitions are not approved for students taking upper division courses before finishing required lower division courses; petitions from frosh, and from transfers who have not completed one semester, are usually denied (except some M.E.T. students) [src: https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/policies-procedures/enrollment/#ac12250 (section "Course load")]

## Breadth

- Humanities and Social Sciences (H/SS): at least six unique H/SS courses; the two Reading and Composition courses count as 2 of the 6 [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- Each H/SS course must be at least 3 semester units (or 4 quarter units) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- At least 2 of the 6 must be upper division (courses numbered 100-196) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- At most 2 of the 6 may be met by AP, IB or A-Level exam scores [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- The six must be unique courses (e.g. AGRS R44 can fill R&C Part A, R&C Part B, or another lower division H/SS slot, but not all at once); a course may also count across University, Campus and College requirements (the catalog's example: HISTORY 127AC satisfies American Cultures and one upper division H/SS course, and 6 H/SS courses are still required) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- The four non-R&C courses may be taken Pass/No Pass and must come from these Class Schedule lists: American History and Institutions; American Cultures (at least 3 units, P or C- or better); the L&S breadth lists Arts & Literature, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy & Values, Social & Behavioral Science; approved foreign language courses [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- American History and Institutions courses count for H/SS when passed with a grade of C or better or P [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- Note — college site differs: CoE's H/SS page says American History and Institutions counts "by passing with a grade of C- or better or P" [src: https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/degree-requirements/humanities-and-social-sciences/]
- Not accepted: courses offered by an Engineering department (except BIOENG 100, COMPSCI C79, ENGIN 125, ENGIN 157AC, ENGIN 184, ENGIN 185, MECENG 191K); courses numbered 97, 98, 99 or above 196; courses only on the L&S Physical Science or Biological Science lists; going abroad by itself [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- Special topics courses of 3 or more units are reviewed case by case; see the ESS Adviser in advance [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]

## Reading & Composition

- Two of the six H/SS courses must fulfill Reading and Composition (Part A and Part B), taken for a letter grade with C- or better [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- Deadlines: Part A by the end of the freshman year (before the 3rd semester); Part B no later than the end of the sophomore year (before the 5th semester) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- Only Part A can be met by an AP, IB or A-Level exam score; exam scores do not fulfill Part B for CoE students [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Humanities and Social Sciences Requirement")]
- A student who has not completed Entry Level Writing must take College Writing R1A (6 units) at UC Berkeley in the first year and earn a C or higher [src: https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/degree-requirements/humanities-and-social-sciences/]

## Other college requirements

- At least 120 semester units and the requirements of one Engineering major [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- GPA: at least 2.000 overall and at least 2.000 in upper division technical coursework required of the major; good standing also expects a semester and cumulative GPA of 2.0 every semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Residence: the final 30 units and two semesters must be completed in residence in the College of Engineering on the Berkeley campus [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Technical courses that fulfill a major requirement must be letter graded (unless only offered P/NP) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Complete the lower division program before enrolling in upper division engineering courses [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Time limit: entering freshmen have at most eight semesters and entering junior transfers at most five; summer terms do not count [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Unit restrictions: at most 16 units of special studies (97, 98, 99, 197, 198, 199) toward the B.S. and no more than 4 in any single term; at most 4 units of Physical Education [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements", "Unit Requirements")]
- Note — college site differs: CoE's enrollment page lists the 16-unit cap as "98, 99, 197, 198 and 199 courses" (no 97), although the same section defines special studies courses as 97, 98, 99, 197, 198 and 199 [src: https://engineering.berkeley.edu/students/undergraduate-guide/policies-procedures/enrollment/#ac12254 (section "Independent, group and field studies courses")]
- CoE's own Passed (P) limit, as the CoE set states it: P grades may be no more than one third of the total units completed at UC Berkeley, FPF, UCEAP or UCDC toward the 120, and transfer credit (including credit-bearing exams) is not factored in; campus.md words the 1/3 Passed Grade Limit on a different base (120 minus transfer and exam units) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/16330U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "COE Unit and Academic Requirements", "Unit Requirements")]
