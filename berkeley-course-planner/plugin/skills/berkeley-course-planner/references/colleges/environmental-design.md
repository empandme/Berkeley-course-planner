# College of Environmental Design (CED)

The catalog states CED college requirements on every CED program page: open the Requirements tab, then click a set name under "College Requirements" to open its text. The links below use the Architecture BA page; any CED major page shows the same sets. The CED Undergraduate Policies Guide is a published Google Doc linked from CED's "Forms and Policies" page.

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 12 units (each fall and spring semester) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Unit Minimum")]
- Maximum per semester: 20.5 units; taking more than 20.5 units needs permission from the major advisor [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Unit Maximum")]
- First-year entrants with no prior units need 15 units per semester to graduate in four years [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Unit Minimum")]
- Note — college site differs: the CED Policies Guide says "CED recommends first-year students begin with 12 units and work towards enrolling in 12-16 units each semester" [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQnoyiZDlZgSwZlpmaLucfzukQ19GvNDElYNLf8zh6KsDTqEmyQXUQMb1USWVSyqXyCsdgWPyYKzNdy/pub (section "Units Loads")]
- Fewer than 12 units needs an approved reduced course load (DSP, employment of 15-30 hours a week, student parents, graduating seniors in their final semester); an unapproved load under 12 brings a registration hold [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQnoyiZDlZgSwZlpmaLucfzukQ19GvNDElYNLf8zh6KsDTqEmyQXUQMb1USWVSyqXyCsdgWPyYKzNdy/pub (section "Units Loads")]
- Over 20.5 units: eligible students have a strong record of A and B grades and at least two completed semesters, one with a high unit total; meet a CED Advisor in the first week; requests are processed from the Monday of week 2; requests to go over 20.5 to hold extra waitlisted courses are denied [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQnoyiZDlZgSwZlpmaLucfzukQ19GvNDElYNLf8zh6KsDTqEmyQXUQMb1USWVSyqXyCsdgWPyYKzNdy/pub (section "Units Loads")]

## Breadth

- Seven-Course Breadth: one course in each of seven areas: Arts and Literature, Biological Science, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy and Values, Physical Science, Social and Behavioral Sciences [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Seven Course Breadth")]
- No more than two courses from any one academic department; up to two CED courses (or courses cross-listed with CED) may count (students admitted Fall 2013 and later) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Seven Course Breadth")]
- Overlap: only lower division ENV DES courses (ENV DES 1, ENV DES 5) and lower division major requirements may also count for breadth; a course used for an upper division major requirement or the upper division CED outside-the-major requirement may not; other upper division CED courses may [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Seven Course Breadth")]
- Grades: a breadth course that also completes another CED requirement needs a letter grade of C- or better; only one of the remaining breadth courses may be taken Passed/Not Passed [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Seven Course Breadth")]
- International Studies may be satisfied by participation in the UC Education Abroad Program or a recognized equivalent program [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Seven Course Breadth")]
- High school exam credit (AP, IB, A-Level) may apply toward many CED requirements; see CED's High School Exam Credit section [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Seven Course Breadth")]
- Breadth courses taken elsewhere must be at least three units each and approved for breadth by the college [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Fulfilling Requirements with Transfer Coursework")]

## Reading & Composition

- Two semesters of lower division composition, Part A and Part B, taken in sequential order [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Every course used for Part A or Part B needs a letter grade of C- or better [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Deadline: all undergraduates should complete Reading and Composition by the end of their fourth semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Note — college site differs: the CED Policies Guide says both halves must be completed by the end of the 4th semester, and students who do not will get a hold preventing enrollment in the following semester [src: https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQnoyiZDlZgSwZlpmaLucfzukQ19GvNDElYNLf8zh6KsDTqEmyQXUQMb1USWVSyqXyCsdgWPyYKzNdy/pub (section "Registration/Enrollment & Applicable Deadlines")]
- A student without Entry Level Writing must enroll in College Writing R1A (6 units), which satisfies Entry Level Writing and Part A; Entry Level Writing needs a C or higher in it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Exams: AP English Literature 4 satisfies Part A and 5 satisfies Parts A and B; AP English Language 4 or 5 satisfies Part A; IB HL English A: Literature 5 to 7 satisfies Parts A and B, and for students admitted fall 2018 or later so does IB HL English A: Language and Literature 5 to 7 (IB SL exams and the IB Literature and Performance exam are not eligible); GCE A-Level English Literature is not accepted [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]

## Other college requirements

- Upper Division Environmental Design: three upper division CED courses from departments outside the major (at least 9 units), each at least 2 units, numbered 100-299 (excluding 197, 198, 199), letter grade C- or better; such a course may not also count for Seven-Course Breadth or the major [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Environmental Design")]
- Units: at least 120; no more than 60 units from a single department count toward the 120 [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Unit Requirements")]
- Semester limit: eight fall/spring semesters for first-year entrants and four for transfers; EAP and UCDC count, summer session, UC Extension and non-UC study abroad do not; CED does not limit total units accrued [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Semester Limit")]
- GPA: 2.0 UC GPA for graduation and 2.0 GPA within the major at graduation [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Grade Requirements")]
- Transfer and study abroad work: up to two courses taken elsewhere each for upper division major requirements, for upper division CED outside the major, and for upper division CED minor requirements [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Fulfilling Requirements with Transfer Coursework")]
- Concurrent enrollment at another school during a fall or spring semester needs CED Advising approval four weeks before the first day of instruction [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/19084U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CED Unit and Academic Requirements", "Concurrent Enrollment")]
