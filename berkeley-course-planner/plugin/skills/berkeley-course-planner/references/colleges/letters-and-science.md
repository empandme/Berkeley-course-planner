# College of Letters & Science (L&S)

The catalog states L&S college requirements on every L&S program page: open the Requirements tab, then click a set name under "College Requirements" to open its text. The links below use the Physics BA page; any L&S major page shows the same sets. The catalog gives no per-semester unit numbers, so the unit limits come from L&S Advising.

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 13 units (Fall or Spring semester) [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/unit-minimum-maximum-semester]
- Maximum per semester: 20.5 units (Fall or Spring semester) [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/unit-minimum-maximum-semester]
- Fewer than 13 units needs an approved Reduced Course Load (RCL); eligible grounds include paid employment of 15 or more hours a week, DSP, caregiver responsibilities, graduating senior in the EGT semester, a major honors program, or an approved late drop; RCL petitions are due by the add/drop deadline in the fourth week [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/unit-minimum-maximum-semester (FAQ accordion "Reduced Course Load (taking below 13 units): Overview and Eligibility" > sub-heading "Eligibility" for the grounds; FAQ accordion "Reduced Course Load: Procedure" for the deadline)]
- An unapproved load under 13 units brings a warning email the first time and a hold on future registration the next time [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/unit-minimum-maximum-semester (FAQ accordion "Reduced Course Load (taking below 13 units): Overview and Eligibility" > sub-heading "Unapproved reduced course load")]
- More than 20.5 units: request permission during the second week of instruction; undeclared students with a 3.3 GPA or higher use an online form, undeclared students below 3.3 meet an L&S adviser, declared students meet their Undergraduate Major Adviser; approval is not guaranteed [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/unit-minimum-maximum-semester (FAQ "Enrolling in more than 20.5 units")]

## Breadth

- Seven-Course Breadth: one approved course in each of seven areas: Arts and Literature, Biological Science, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy and Values, Physical Science, Social and Behavioral Sciences [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Each course needs a minimum grade of C- or P [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Seven distinct courses are required; a course listed in several areas may be used for only one of them [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Only college courses count; exams such as AP and IB cannot be used for breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- At most two courses from any one academic department, counting all of that department's subject areas and, for a cross-listed ("C") course, every department offering it; Letters and Science ("LS") courses are exempt from this limit [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- Note — college site differs: L&S Advising states the limit as a maximum of two classes from a single "department or department cluster" [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/degree-requirements (FAQ "May I take one course to satisfy multiple requirements?")]
- Courses that fulfill American History & Institutions, American Cultures, or major requirements may also count toward Seven-Course Breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]
- IGETC Certification or UC Reciprocity satisfies Seven-Course Breadth; a transfer course needs C- or better (or the host campus's Passed equivalent) and at least 3.0 semester or 4.0 quarter units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Seven-Course Breadth")]

## Reading & Composition

- Two semesters of lower division composition, Part A and Part B, taken in sequential order [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Every course used for Part A or Part B needs a letter grade of C- or better [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Part A must be completed before Part B; the two cannot be taken at the same time [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/reading-composition]
- Deadline: all undergraduates should complete Reading and Composition by the end of their fourth semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Note — college site differs: L&S Advising says students who started as first-years must complete it by the end of the fourth semester or their enrollment may be blocked [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/reading-composition]
- A student without Entry Level Writing must enroll in COLWRIT R1A (6 units), which satisfies Entry Level Writing and Part A; Entry Level Writing needs a C or higher in it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- AGRS R44 with C- or higher may satisfy either Part A or Part B and may also count for Arts and Literature, Historical Studies, or Social & Behavioral Sciences breadth; no other Reading and Composition course may count toward Seven-Course Breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Exams: AP English Literature 4 satisfies Part A and 5 satisfies Parts A and B; AP English Language 4 or 5 satisfies Part A; IB HL English A: Literature 5 to 7 satisfies Parts A and B, and for students admitted fall 2018 or later so does IB HL English A: Language and Literature 5 to 7 (IB SL exams and the IB Literature and Performance exam are not eligible); GCE A-Level English Literature is not accepted [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]

## Other college requirements

- Quantitative Reasoning: one approved course or a qualifying exam score [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Quantitative Reasoning")]
- Quantitative Reasoning course: C- or better, letter graded only [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/quantitative-reasoning]
- Language: proficiency in a language other than English equivalent to the second-semester college level, by exam or by an approved course [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Language Requirement")]
- Language by Berkeley course: a second-semester or higher course in a language other than English with a letter grade of C- or higher, or a pre-approved breadth course taught in that language that requires an advanced language course as a prerequisite (C- or higher letter grade; it may also count for one Seven-Course Breadth area) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Language Requirement")]
- Language: IGETC Certification or UC Reciprocity satisfies it; CalGETC certification does not [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Language Requirement")]
- Units: 120 total; 36 of them upper division; of those 36, 6 must be in courses offered outside the major department and 18 must be taken in residence [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S Unit Requirements")]
- Upper division residence: 12 of the 18 upper division units taken in residence must be in the major program [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/degree-requirements]
- Unit restrictions: no more than 16 units of courses numbered 97, 98, 99, 197, 198 or 199; no more than 4 units of PE activity courses; no more than 6 units of courses numbered 300-499 [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/degree-requirements]
- L&S senior residence: after 90 units, at least 24 units and at least 2 semesters in residence as an L&S student, each semester with at least 6 units passed; one Berkeley Summer Session of 6 units may count as one of the 2 semesters [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/degree-requirements]
- Courses for the major must be letter graded, except courses offered Pass/No Pass only; a C (2.0) GPA in all major work and a C (2.00) average in upper division major courses [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S General Guidelines")]
- Overlap: no more than one upper division course may count for both a major and a minor, and no more than two for a double major [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/25666U/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "L&S General Guidelines")]
- Semesters allowed: eight for students who entered as first-years, four for transfers (Fall and Spring only); more semesters depend on the unit ceiling of 130 units (single major) or 136 units (double major or more) [src: https://lsadvising.berkeley.edu/policies/unit-ceiling-and-semester-limit]
