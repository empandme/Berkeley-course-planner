# College of Computing, Data Science, and Society (CDSS)

The catalog states CDSS college requirements on every CDSS program page: open the Requirements tab, then click a set name under "College Requirements" to open its text. The links below use the Data Science BA page; any CDSS major page shows the same sets.

## Unit limits

- Minimum per semester: 12 units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Unit and Academic Requirements", "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- Maximum per semester: 20.5 units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Unit and Academic Requirements", "Class Schedule Requirements")]
- Fewer than 12 units needs an approved Reduced Course Load; eligible grounds include paid employment of 15 or more hours a week (minimum 6 units), DSP, parenthood/caregiver (2 courses totalling 6 units), graduating senior in the EGT semester, a major honors program, or an approved late drop; the request form is due by the 3rd Friday of the semester [src: https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/policies/unit-semester-limits (tab "Minimum Units")]
- More than 20.5 units: request permission during the second week of instruction with the CDSS Maximum Unit Increase Request form; approval is not guaranteed, and an approved student must still add the course before the add/drop deadline [src: https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/policies/unit-semester-limits (tab "Maximum Units")]

## Breadth

- Seven-Course Breadth: one approved course in each of seven areas: Arts and Literature, Biological Science, Historical Studies, International Studies, Philosophy and Values, Physical Science, Social and Behavioral Sciences [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- Each course needs a minimum grade of C- or P [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- Seven distinct courses are required; a course listed in several areas may be used for only one of them [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- At most two courses from any one academic department, counting all of that department's subject areas and, for a cross-listed ("C") course, every department offering it; Letters and Science ("LS") courses are exempt from this limit [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- Courses that fulfill American History & Institutions, American Cultures, or major requirements may also count toward Seven-Course Breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- Only college courses count; exams such as AP and IB cannot be used for breadth [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- Same as L&S breadth except that a second-semester foreign language course can be used for International Studies [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- A transfer course needs C- or better (or the host campus's Passed equivalent) and at least 3.0 semester or 4.0 quarter units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Breadth")]
- IGETC completion satisfies all Seven-Course Breadth for transfer students [src: https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/college-degree-requirements-and-policies#section-el-degree-requirements-1 (section "College")]

## Reading & Composition

- Two semesters of lower division composition, Part A and Part B, taken in sequential order [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Every course used for Part A or Part B needs a letter grade of C- or better [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Deadline: all undergraduates should complete Reading and Composition by the end of their fourth semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- A student without Entry Level Writing must enroll in College Writing R1A (6 units), which satisfies Entry Level Writing and Part A; Entry Level Writing needs a C or higher in it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- Exams: AP English Literature 4 satisfies Part A and 5 satisfies Parts A and B; AP English Language 4 or 5 satisfies Part A; IB HL English A: Literature 5 to 7 satisfies Parts A and B, and for students admitted fall 2018 or later so does IB HL English A: Language and Literature 5 to 7 (IB SL exams and the IB Literature and Performance exam are not eligible); GCE A-Level English Literature is not accepted [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "Reading and Composition")]
- IGETC completion satisfies Reading and Composition for transfer students [src: https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/college-degree-requirements-and-policies#section-el-degree-requirements-1 (section "College")]

## Other college requirements

- Computational Reasoning: one approved course with a letter grade of C- or higher, or an exam (AP Computer Science Principles 3 to 5; IB HL Computer Science 5 to 7); the Computer Science and Data Science majors satisfy it through their major requirements [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Computational Reasoning")]
- Statistical Reasoning: one approved course with a letter grade of C- or higher, or an exam (AP Statistics 3 to 5; IB HL Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation 5 to 7; GCE A-level Mathematics in Probability and Statistics A to C); the Statistics and Data Science majors satisfy it through their major requirements [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Statistical Reasoning")]
- COMPSCI/DATA/STAT C8 can satisfy either Computational Reasoning or Statistical Reasoning, but not both [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Statistical Reasoning")]
- Human and Social Dynamics of Data and Technology: satisfied by taking an approved course, with a letter grade of C- or higher; the Data Science major satisfies it through its major requirements; no California Community College course has been identified for it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Human and Social Dynamics of Data and Technology")]
- Units: 120 total, at least 36 upper division; at most 4 PE units, 16 Special Studies units and 6 units of courses numbered 300-499 [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- GPA: minimum cumulative 2.0, minimum 1.5 for one semester, and 2.0 in upper division and graduate courses identified for the major [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Semester limit: 8 for first-year entrants, 5 for transfer students; summer terms do not count [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/programs/A50AMU/requirements-krhha (College Requirements > "CDSS Unit and Academic Requirements")]
- Senior residence: after 90 units, at least 24 units and at least 2 terms in residence as a CDSS student, each term with at least 6 units passed [src: https://cdss.berkeley.edu/academics/college-degree-requirements-and-policies#section-el-degree-requirements-1 (section "College")]
