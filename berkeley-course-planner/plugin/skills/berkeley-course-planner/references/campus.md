# Campus requirements

These apply to every undergraduate in every college. The student's college file adds that college's own rules. Each bullet says only what its source page says; a click path in brackets tells you where on the page to look.

## Entry Level Writing

- Required of every student who enters the University of California as a first-year student; students admitted as transfers have satisfied it through the English composition courses completed for transfer admission eligibility [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/entry-level-writing]
- Three ways to satisfy it: a standardized exam score, an English composition course, or the Berkeley Writing Assessment (BWA); first-year students who have not satisfied it before fall enrollment may take a make-up BWA in their first semester [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/entry-level-writing]
- Once a student begins courses at Berkeley without having met it otherwise, the student must complete the 6-unit COLWRIT R1A; a grade of C or higher satisfies both Entry Level Writing and Part A of Reading and Composition [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/entry-level-writing]

## American History & Institutions

- High school: a yearlong United States history course with C- or higher, or a half-year United States history course and a half-year American government course with C- or higher [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/american-history-american-institutions]
- Exams: AP United States History or AP African American Studies score of 3, 4 or 5; IB History of the Americas HL score of 5, 6 or 7 [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/american-history-american-institutions]
- College course: an approved UC Berkeley course, or an acceptable UC-transferable course of at least 3 semester or 4 quarter units, with C- or higher or P (Passed) [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/american-history-american-institutions]
- Nonimmigrant visa exemption: students with a current nonimmigrant visa status (F is the most common) are exempt once they complete 90.5 units, as long as the visa status is maintained through the last day of finals for the fall or spring semester, or the last day of Summer Sessions instruction in August [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/american-history-american-institutions]
- Students with a nonimmigrant visa status who plan to apply for residency while at Berkeley are advised to complete the requirement so graduation is not delayed if residency is approved before the final term [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/american-history-american-institutions]

## American Cultures

- Pass one American Cultures course with a grade not lower than C- or P; all undergraduates need it to graduate [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/american-cultures]
- The course may be taken at any time during the undergraduate career; American Cultures courses are offered at both lower and upper division level [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/american-cultures]
- A course number with the suffix AC satisfies the American Cultures requirement [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/policies/units-courses]

## Unit rules

- Minimum per semester: no campus-wide number; minimum course load requirements vary by college or school. A course load of 15 units per semester is considered normal [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/policies/degree-progress]
- Maximum per semester: no campus-wide number; during the Adjustment Period students may enroll up to their college's maximum unit limit [src: https://registrar.berkeley.edu/enrollment/]
- Above the maximum: the Registrar's Enrollment page says that during the Adjustment Period students may enroll up to their college's maximum unit limit, and it states no campus-wide procedure for going above that limit [src: https://registrar.berkeley.edu/enrollment/]
- Below the minimum: a student who enrolls in fewer units than the minimum needs the schedule approved by the dean of the college or school [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/policies/degree-progress]
- Enrollment phase caps: new students are limited to 17.5 units in Phase 1 and get no Phase 2; continuing undergraduates are limited to 13.5 units in Phase 1 and 17.5 units in Phase 2; the caps include waitlisted units [src: https://registrar.berkeley.edu/enrollment/]
- The Adjustment Period is in August (fall semester) and January (spring semester), and it ends on the Friday of the tenth week of instruction [src: https://registrar.berkeley.edu/enrollment/]
- Special Studies per semester: no more than 4 units of courses numbered 97, 98, 99, 197, 198 and 199 in a single semester; the dean of the college or school may grant exceptions [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/policies/units-courses]
- Minimum progress: completed units must be no fewer than 15 times the number of semesters enrolled at Berkeley, less one; summer session is not counted as a semester; normal progress is 30 units a year [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/policies/degree-progress]
- Degree total: at least 120 semester units; no more than 4 PE units and 16 Special Studies units (97, 98, 99, 197, 198, 199) count toward it [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/units-gpa]
- 1/3 Passed Grade Limit: up to one-third of the units toward the 120 may be Passed (P) units, computed on 120 minus transfer and exam units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/units-gpa]
- Senior Residence: after reaching 90 units, earn at least 24 more units through UC Berkeley courses over at least two terms, each term with at least 6 earned units [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/units-gpa]
- Minimum overall GPA of 2.00 for graduation [src: https://undergraduate.catalog.berkeley.edu/earning-your-degree/university-campus-requirements/units-gpa]
